## Brand & Style

The brand personality is witty, self-aware, warm, and unapologetically celebratory. It balances the elaborate, sacred gravity of a traditional Bengali wedding (Biye) with affectionate satire of matchmaking tropes: "BCS Cadre Groom", "Sundor-Shushil Bride", overbearing aunties, horoscope matching, and exaggerated biodatas. The emotional response is lighthearted delight, trust through humor, and cultural nostalgia.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile / Editorial Folk** with **Quirky Neo-Traditionalism**. Surfaces resemble warm handmade parchment and wedding invitation paper (Kalo/Lal Biye Card feel). Visuals feature crisp kolka filigrees, hand-stamped wax or rubber seal badges, punchy dialogue balloons, and dynamic festive contrasts. While the humor is bold and playful, the functional matchmaking mechanics remain crisp, accessible, and high-converting.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from the vibrancy of a Bengali wedding p貌ndel, turmeric paste, and crimson vermilion:

- **Primary (`#9B111E` - Crimson Sindoor):** The commanding royal crimson. Used for primary CTAs, critical badges, prominent headline accents, and celebratory borders.
- **Secondary (`#E5A910` - Marigold / Genda Phool):** Radiant golden amber. Used for feature highlights, rating stars, playful seals, and festive glows.
- **Tertiary (`#137547` - Jamdani Emerald):** A rich, grounded forest emerald. Evokes betel leaves (paan pata) and traditional weaves. Used for verified status badges, biodata success metrics, and secondary actions.
- **Accent Pink (`#E84373` - Alpona Magenta):** A punchy playful pink used sparingly for romantic micro-interactions, speech bubble borders, and satirical quirk tags.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF6EE` - Patachitra Cream):** Warm, unbleached handmade paper tone providing high-legibility contrast without the harsh glare of stark white.
- **Neutral Dark (`#231713` - Kajal Brown-Black):** Deep charcoal-sepia for high-contrast, comfortable reading of complex biodatas.

## Typography

The typography unites structural Latin fonts with natural rhythm and wide open counters that harmonize perfectly with Bengali script pairings (such as Tiro Bangla, Hind Siliguri, or Kalpurush).

- **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Expressive, geometric, and sturdy. It mimics the punchy cadence of wedding headlines, vintage Indian press advertisements, and comic speech titles.
- **Body (`Work Sans`):** Clean, open, exceptionally legible at dense biodata specifications, astrological charts, and long humorous testimonials.
- **Multilingual Behavior:** When rendering Bengali script, fallback strictly inherits fonts with equal vertical metrics (e.g., `Hind Siliguri`, `Tiro Bangla`) with increased line-height (+4px) to accommodate matras and vowel ligatures without clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display cards, filtering drawers, and dense matrimonial checklists side-by-side:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2.5rem` outer canvas margin. Max container width capped at `1280px` for optimal viewing of biodatas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `1.5rem` outer canvas margin. Filter sidebars collapse into a floating bottom drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` outer canvas margin. Biodata profiles reflow to single-column swipeable cards.

Spacing is metered in strict multiples of `4px`/`8px`. Generous vertical padding (`space-xl` to `3rem`) is reserved for editorial section breaks styled with floral alpona/kolka dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews sterile, hyper-modern drop shadows in favor of a tactile, printed-paper look:

- **Low-Tier Elevation (Cards, Filters):** 1px solid border (`#EADCC6`) paired with a hard, warm ambient offset shadow: `2px 3px 0px rgba(35, 23, 19, 0.08)`.
- **Medium-Tier Elevation (Speech Bubbles, Hover States):** `3px 4px 0px #231713`. This delivers a punchy comic-book weight without veering into stark brutalism.
- **High-Tier Elevation (Modals, Match Seals, Dialogues):** `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(155, 17, 30, 0.16), 4px 6px 0px #231713`.
- **Folk Stamps & Certificates:** Slightly rotated surfaces (`-1.5deg` to `+2deg`) combined with stamped ink transparency effects to evoke authentic matchmaker rubber-stamps (e.g., "100% Ghoroa Approved", "Aunty Certified").

## Shapes

The roundedness level is **Rounded** (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for large cards, `1.5rem` for modals). 

- **Speech Bubbles:** Feature an integrated bottom-left or bottom-right triangular tail indicator (8px-12px) with curved vertices.
- **Stamps & Seals:** Scalloped circular or 8-point starburst edges with dotted or dash-offset borders.
- **Motif Inlays:** Traditional kolka (paisley-flourish) corner cuts or arch cutouts (mimicking thakur dalan arches) on hero containers and premium matrimonial bios.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Biye Koro CTA):** Solid `#9B111E` (Crimson) fill, `#FAF6EE` label, `0.5rem` radius, with a bold `1.5px` border in `#231713` and a 2px offset solid drop-shadow. Hover triggers a slight lift (`translateY(-2px)`) with expanded shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Dekhi Shuni):** `#FAF6EE` background, `#9B111E` text, `1.5px` border in `#9B111E`.
- **Tertiary/Ghost (Ghotok Advice):** Text-only with an animated henna/kolka underline stroke on hover.

### Badges & Satirical Stamps
- **Rubber Stamp Certifications:** Rotated badges (e.g., "100% Non-Smoker", "Golap Jamun Lover", "Canada PR Ready") with dashed double-borders and distressed stamp-like color tints.
- **Status Pills:** Compact rounded pills with emerald (`#137547`) or gold (`#E5A910`) tints featuring micro-icons (topor, shankha, fish motif).

### Cards & Speech Bubbles
- **Biodata Match Card:** Off-white cream surface with a warm border, high-contrast photo container framed with a subtle gold arch, quick tags (Height, Career, District), and a quick "Pathao Prostab" (Send Proposal) button.
- **Comic Speech Bubbles:** Pillowed containers with borders and tail carats holding quotes from fictional aunties, satisfied mothers-in-law, and candid grooms.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Generous `44px` height, bordered in `#D4C2A5`, resting on `#FFFFFF`. On focus, shifts to `#9B111E` border with a 2px marigold-tinted ring (`rgba(229, 169, 16, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-accentuated feel; radio buttons feature a central solid dot evoking a crimson bindi or kumkum dot.

### Specialized Matchmaking Components
- **"Ghotok Rating" Scale:** A humorous five-point rating bar indicated by topors (groom headgear) or sweets instead of ordinary stars.
- **Horoscope & Requirement Matrix:** Playful tabular cards showing "Shasuri Approved" (Mother-in-law approved) criteria with playful check/cross indicators.
